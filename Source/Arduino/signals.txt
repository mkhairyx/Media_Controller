Signals:

- Left:
Send with: IrSender.sendNEC(0x4, 0x7, <numberOfRepeats>);
--
- Right:
Send with: IrSender.sendNEC(0x4, 0x6, <numberOfRepeats>);
--
- Up:
Send with: IrSender.sendNEC(0x4, 0x40, <numberOfRepeats>);
--
- Down:
Send with: IrSender.sendNEC(0x4, 0x41, <numberOfRepeats>);
--
- Ok:
Send with: IrSender.sendNEC(0x4, 0x44, <numberOfRepeats>);

- Volume Up:
Send with: IrSender.sendNEC(0x4, 0x2, <numberOfRepeats>);

- Volume Down:
Send with: IrSender.sendNEC(0x4, 0x3, <numberOfRepeats>);
